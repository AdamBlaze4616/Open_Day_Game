AskPAI Capstone — Policy AI

Working on AskPAI, an AI-powered policy assistant designed around trustworthy retrieval, citations, secure ingestion and controlled answers. The project combines cybersecurity, AI, RAG, ChromaDB and Python with a strong focus on reducing hallucinations, protecting stored data and ensuring answers remain grounded in official policy sources.

Outside university work, I have a strong personal interest in scambaiting and scam prevention.
