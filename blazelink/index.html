Research & writing

Co-writer on Melbourne Companions research.

I was a co-writer of a research paper documenting Melbourne Companions and the project’s approach to community cyber and AI safety.

Melbourne Companions research paper
Learn, Ask, Connect: Turning University Cyber and AI Knowledge into Community Impact
Mary George • Adam Mardiryan • David Bonnett • Ricardo Krestevski • Associate Professor Leanne Ngo

The paper examines how students, academics and community partners can work together to deliver cyber and AI safety support that can adapt to different communities while developing students’ practical skills. Melbourne Companions combines structured education with direct conversation and personalised support rather than relying only on one-way presentations.

~50older community members received direct support in the first City of Kingston program
100+people were involved across the wider initiative
A$6,000additional 2026 funding supporting further delivery and expansion

Learn

Practical education around scams, phishing, safe online practices, AI-generated content and deepfakes.

Ask

Space for community members to ask about the risks that matter to them and discuss suspicious online content directly with trained volunteers.

Connect

Direct connection with trained university students, including personalised and multilingual support where possible.

Adapt

Delivery can change its language, examples, priority risks, resources and volunteer preparation to better suit each community.

My contribution: I contributed as a co-writer while also being involved in the cybersecurity and community-facing work surrounding Melbourne Companions. The paper connects practical delivery with lessons about accessible communication, privacy boundaries, cultural awareness, multilingual support and responsible community cyber education.

Melbourne Companions — Cyber & AI Safety

Through collaboration involving Enactus La Trobe, LTUCS and WiCyS, I support cyber and AI safety education for older and underserved community members. Topics include scams, phishing, impersonation, deepfakes, AI-generated content, privacy, passwords and safer digital habits.

🛡

Scambaiting & Social Engineering Defence

I independently study scam campaigns and attacker behaviour to understand the manipulation techniques behind online fraud. I use ethical scambaiting and analysis to learn how scammers establish trust, create urgency and pressure victims, then turn those lessons into practical awareness and prevention guidance.
